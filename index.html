1. Information We Collect

The Calculator App is designed to perform basic and advanced mathematical calculations entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

a. No Personal Data Collected

We do not require you to provide any personal information (e.g., name, email, phone number).

We do not collect usage data, calculation history, or any inputs you enter into the calculator.

All calculations are processed locally on your device and are never sent to our servers or any third party.

b. Automatically Collected Information

The App does not collect any device information, IP addresses, location data, or analytics.

c. Permissions

The App requires no special permissions (e.g., camera, microphone, location, storage, or contacts).

2. How We Use Information

Since we do not collect any information, there is no use, sharing, or storage of data.

3. Third-Party Services

The Calculator App does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics providers, advertising networks, or social media platforms.

No Ads: The App is ad-free.

No Tracking: No cookies, pixels, or tracking technologies are used.

No Data Sharing: We do not share data with any third parties because no data is collected.

4. Data Security

Your calculations are processed exclusively on your device. No data leaves your phone or tablet. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect the App from unauthorized access, but since no data is transmitted or stored remotely, there is no server-side risk.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. Since no personal information is collected, the App fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

6. International Data Transfers

No data is transferred, stored, or processed outside your device, so international data transfer regulations (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) do not apply.

7. Your Data Rights

Because we collect no data, you have no data to access, correct, delete, or export. Your calculation history (if any) is stored only in the App's local memory and can be cleared by uninstalling the App or clearing app data through your device settings.

By using the Calculator App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

This App is 100% offline, private, and secure by design.
